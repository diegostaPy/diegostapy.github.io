About me

I am a computational scientist and researcher at the Faculty of Engineering of the National University of Asunción. My work connects astrophysics, high-performance computing and artificial intelligence to address challenges in energy, environment and public health.

I have been Vice Dean of FIUNA since September 1, 2025, while continuing to teach, coordinate academic programs and lead interdisciplinary projects.
