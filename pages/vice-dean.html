Government plan and action tracking

Core priorities

Full transparency, meaningful participation and verifiable actions for the entire FIUNA community.

Open government and accountability

Commitment: full transparency and public accountability.

  • Open Council, livestreamed sessions and public minutes.
  • Digital procedures through the MECIP system.
  • Participatory faculty assemblies and a weekly Open Government Day.
  • Public tracking of every plan action.

For the community: a real space where every voice counts.

Critical courses and student support

Commitment: no student should be lost because the system does not support them.

  • Identify high-retention courses by degree.
  • Critical Courses Committee and early-warning system.
  • Mandatory tutoring and integrative projects.
  • Annual curriculum updates with AI, renewable energy and Industry 5.0.

For students: real support and on-time graduation.

Self-evaluation and accreditation

Commitment: restore and sustain academic quality.

  • Specific accreditation and monitoring strategy.
  • Contingency plan for Civil Engineering reassessment.
  • Continuous improvement and permanent self-evaluation with all groups.

For graduates: degrees with prestige and market value.

Industry partnerships and joint projects

Commitment: connect FIUNA with the real economy.

  • Company agreements, technical visits and student talks.
  • R&D&I services portfolio.
  • AI-powered Graduate Employability Observatory.
  • Government and industry alliances through graduates.

For graduates: new opportunities and direct industry links.

Technology transfer

Commitment: FIUNA as a driver of innovation and productive development.

  • Technology Transfer Office.
  • Co-funded FIUNA–Industry projects.
  • Intellectual property, licensing and a startup incubator.
  • Alumni links to identify opportunities.

For companies: a strategic partner for real-world solutions.

Graduate studies and international links

Commitment: excellent education with global reach.

  • More diplomas, master's and doctoral programs.
  • Double degrees and international mobility.
  • International curriculum and stronger research groups.
  • Suitable graduate-study infrastructure.

For faculty and graduates: world-class training at home.

Statute reform

Commitment: a new university model for the bicentennial.

  • Participatory modernization of UNA governance.
  • Alignment with accreditation and international rankings.
  • New participation and decision-making mechanisms.

For the community: a modern, transparent university.

Research and institutional participation

Commitment: research as a differentiator for innovation.

  • Research seedbeds from the first years.
  • Innovation Lab for startups and spin-offs.
  • Support for indexed conferences and flexibility for scientific output.
  • Fair resource allocation across degrees.

Dialogue with faculty

Commitment: change is built with everyone.

  • Regular meetings by degree and classroom visits.
  • Merit-based faculty competitions with public calendars.
  • Participatory assemblies and analysis of area-based course assignments.

For faculty: a voice that is heard and valued.
